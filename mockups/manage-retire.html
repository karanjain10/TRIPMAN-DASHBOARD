Retire Truck 12

The unit is taken out of service for good: it is cleared from today's deployment and cannot be activated or deployed until someone un-retires it. It is not deleted, and its trips and history stay.

Rules

Who sees the buttons: admins and owners only, the same people the server allows (it refuses everyone else).
Records: the server writes a RETIRED / UNRETIRED line in the equipment status log with who and why. It is not counted as a breakdown.
Maintenance screen and Equipment Health: a retired unit is not listed as "Down now" (today it would show up there with no reason, because retiring also switches it inactive).
Un-retire: brings it back as inactive; use Activate afterwards to put it in service.
