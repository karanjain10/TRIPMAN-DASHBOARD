How it is worked out

Time per trip = the gap between one trip of a tipper and its next trip, which is one full round (load, haul, dump, return). Every trip carries only one time stamp, so this is the only way to get a duration.
Per excavator = take those gaps for all tippers currently on that excavator, this shift only, and show the median of all of them together (5 tippers means one pool of gaps from all 5).
Shown when there are at least 5 gaps, otherwise "—" (like the per-hour figure, which waits for 30 minutes of shift). Moves with the shift, the same as the trips and per-hour numbers. Hover shows "median of N trips".
Not changed: the pace card, silence alerts, sound, and the pop-up.
